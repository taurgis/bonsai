### 6.5 Effortless bug reports

✅ \*\*Do:\*\*
Make it an effortless task to submit bug reports by providing a URL to open an issue and prepopulating the required data as much as possible. \[Issue templates, like on GitHub\], allow to further guide the users as to which information is necessary.

❌ \*\*Otherwise:\*\*
Users get frustrated searching for how to report a bug and may end up with little helpful information, or not submitting an issue at all.
